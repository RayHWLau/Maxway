English Title: Crack IELTS Task 2 Discussion Essays in 3 Steps

English Voiceover Script for HeyGen
Approx. 40-50 seconds

What is the hardest part of an IELTS Task 2 discussion essay?
It is trying to discuss both sides without losing focus.

But here is a simple way to do it.
Just remember three steps.

Step one: explain Viewpoint A.
Ask yourself three questions:
Why do some people think this way?
How does this idea work in real life?
Who benefits the most?

Step two: explain Viewpoint B.
Ask the same three questions again.
Why do people support this side?
How does it work in practice?
And who gains the most?

Make sure you give both sides equal attention.

Step three: give your own opinion.
Which side do you agree with?
Why do you agree with it?
Be clear, and explain your reason.

So remember: one, two, three.
That is how you handle an IELTS Task 2 discussion essay.
